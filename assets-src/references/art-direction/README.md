These images were generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on

## `environment-concept.png`

Purpose: settle the environment's emotional center, palette relationships,
lighting, elevation language, and balance of rebuilding versus ruins.

Prompt:

> Create an original art-direction concept for a top-down 2D pixel-art game
> called Dark War. Use the attached images collectively only as visual
> references for composition, color, atmospheric lighting, and painterly pixel
> clustering; do not copy any one image or recognizable design. Scene: a
> cheerful post-apocalyptic settlement being rebuilt in a lush valley.
> Orthographic top-down three-quarter gameplay view with clear
> 32-pixel-tile-like spatial readability. Show three stepped grassy elevations
> with hand-shaped warm clay cliff faces, inner and outer corners, a stair cut
> into the cliff, a bright turquoise river widening into a pond, a small wooden
> bridge, a cave entrance, reclaimed stone ruins covered in flowers and vines,
> a colorful patched workshop/home, garden plots, saplings, construction
> materials, a friendly utility robot and a tiny explorer-builder. The world
> should feel cultivated, playful, safe, and full of possibility, with danger
> only implied far away. Visual language: outline-free modern pixel art; no
> black contour lines; shapes separated by hue and value. Painterly
> cluster-based rendering with intentional chunky pixel groups, minimal
> single-pixel noise, expressive organic silhouettes, and readable gameplay
> forms. Limited cohesive palette with saturated leaf greens, mint, turquoise
> and sky-blue water, warm ochre/clay/coral architecture, lavender accents, and
> very deep teal/navy colored shadows. Vibrant, rich, cheerful colors with
> strong but friendly contrast. Warm late-afternoon sun from upper left, cool
> teal ambient shadows, dappled canopy light, subtle atmospheric depth.
> Cartoonish proportions and charming details. Polished game key art that still
> looks achievable as a modular tileset. Avoid: dark grim tone, horror, gore,
> military dominance, photorealism, smooth vector art, heavy black outlines,
> muddy brown-gray palette, UI, labels, text, logos, watermark, isometric
> projection. Landscape 16:9 composition.

## `character-concept.png`

Purpose: align character scale, silhouettes, personality, props, and color with
the environment board.

Prompt:

> Create an original production-facing character and prop art-direction board
> for the top-down 2D pixel-art game Dark War. Use the attached images
> collectively as references for simplicity, cheerful proportions, color
> relationships, and the existing game's subject categories only. Do not copy
> any character, composition, or recognizable design. Present a clean lineup on
> a warm pale mint studio background with no labels or text. Include: a
> resourceful explorer-builder protagonist with a colorful patched jacket and
> tool belt; a friendly compact utility robot with a screen-like face; a
> cheerful scruffy dog companion; a quirky mushroom-like mutant gardener; a
> broad but friendly reclaimed-security robot; a comical oversized beetle
> threat; and a small set of matching props—a watering can, hammer, seed pouch,
> salvaged battery, flower pot, and modular construction crate. Show each figure
> in a readable top-down three-quarter gameplay pose, with a few small
> expression/action variants where space allows. Characters should feel
> suitable for roughly 32x40 to 32x48 pixel sprites, with exaggerated heads,
> compact bodies, expressive gestures, and unmistakable silhouettes. Visual
> language: outline-free modern pixel art, no black contour lines. Use colored
> edge separation and deep teal/navy shadows. Painterly cluster-based rendering
> with intentional chunky pixel groups and minimal isolated pixel noise.
> Cohesive limited palette: saturated leaf green and mint, turquoise and sky
> blue, warm ochre and clay, coral red, sunny gold, lavender, cream highlights,
> deep jewel-tone shadows. Warm upper-left atmospheric light and cool ambient
> shadow. Cheerful, funny, toy-like, approachable post-apocalyptic design
> emphasizing repair, gardening, friendship, and building rather than warfare.
> Avoid: grimdark, gore, realistic soldiers, tactical military uniforms,
> generic fantasy armor, harsh black outlines, washed-out colors, smooth vector
> rendering, photorealism, UI frames, labels, text, logos, watermark,
> sprite-sheet grid lines. Landscape composition.

The generated compositions contain more detail than a 32-pixel gameplay asset
can carry. Production artists must simplify them according to
`docs/ART-DIRECTION.md`; do not crop characters or terrain directly into the
runtime atlas.
